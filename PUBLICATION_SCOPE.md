# Scope and scientific limitations

This is a **computational feasibility research release**, not a fabricated QPU.
- 108/108 unrestricted repository tests passed in a cached FEniCSx Docker environment.
- Seven published model modules have a separate reproducible 42-test suite.
- Thermal magnons do not by themselves supply a coherent microwave drive.
- No measured diamond–magnon chip, experimentally calibrated conversion rate,
  reproducible heat-assisted quantum gate, or quantum advantage is claimed.
- 2.87 GHz screening uses a selected two-level NV transition; not the full spin-1 manifold.
- Existing NV-YIG magnon sensing and room-temperature coherent magnon-spin logic are prior art.
- The proposed physical architecture needs externally sourced T1/T2, film
  linewidth, frequency-resolved PSD and an independently controlled experiment.
- Engineering drawings, layered images and 3D+temperature models are conceptual.
- Source release excludes private research ledger, original complete project code
  and cloud secrets. Public disclosure may affect future patent applications.
